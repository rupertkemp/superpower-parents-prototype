Sample HeyGen Avatar Script — "After-School Meltdowns" (Est. 2 min 50 sec) 
Hi, I'm Dr. Kate Ellen. If your child holds it together all day at school—and the teacher says they're an angel—only for them to explode the moment they get into the car or walk through the front door, please take a deep breath. You are not doing anything wrong.

What you're seeing is something psychologists call after-school restraint collapse. All day long, your child has been using up their nervous system's energy to sit still, follow rules, navigate friendships, and manage sensory noise. By 3:15 PM, their emotional tank is completely empty. And because you are their safe person, they finally let go with you.

Here are three evidence-based strategies you can start today:

First: Create a 20-minute decompression buffer before any questions or chores. Skip "How was your day?" and instead offer a quiet snack, water, and low-demand connection.

Second: Regulate before you educate. When a meltdown hits, the thinking brain is offline. Lower your voice, validate the feeling—"Your day was really big and your body feels overloaded"—and wait until their breathing settles before problem-solving.

Third: Use the downloadable After-School Reset Checklist below this video to spot their early overload cues and build a predictable afternoon rhythm.

Remember: when someone sees beneath the surface, a child's whole world can change. Save this video to your child's folder so you can revisit it anytime.
